## 1. Purpose and evidentiary boundary

This report describes the teaching and service contributions that ISO can observe for the Department of Philosophy and Religion during the complete 2023–24 academic cycle. It is descriptive, not evaluative.

The report also presents each faculty member's **FY26 beginning and final LHE expectations** and explicit adjustment or special role recorded in Carolyn Davis's workbook. Those expectations apply to **2025–26**, two academic years after the observed teaching period. They are later institutional context only. This report does not back-project them onto 2023–24, divide observed teaching by expected LHE, calculate observed-to-expected ratios, or infer workload adequacy, productivity, fairness, quality, or institutional value.

Three evidence scopes remain distinct:

1. The **departmental instructional portfolio** includes every observed PHIL and RSTD section in the 2023–24 cycle, regardless of the instructor's current workforce status or governed home unit.
2. The **current faculty teaching table** includes instruction attributed through governed identity resolution to the 13 current full-time department faculty.
3. The **LHE context table** transcribes later institutional expectations and adjustments from the FY26 workbook.

No authoritative 2023–24 Academic Standing Committees workbook or governed 2023–24 appointment registry is present in the repository. Accordingly, this report describes observed cross-unit service teaching but does not substitute a later committee roster for missing same-year governance evidence.

Section and SCH totals are cumulative across the selected fall, spring, May, and summer terms. PHIL/RSTD offerings occur in Fall 2023, Spring 2024, May Term 2024, Summer Term 1 2024, and Extended Summer 2024; no PHIL/RSTD offering is observed in Summer Term II 2024. Independent studies and practica can carry low enrollment or one credit and still count as sections.

## 2. Department overview

The governed department structure identifies Philosophy and Religion as the owner of the **PHIL** and **RSTD** instructional prefixes. Across the complete 2023–24 instructional cycle, the observed PHIL/RSTD portfolio contains **101 sections, 2,009 enrollments, and 6,011 SCH**.

The 13 current full-time department faculty account for **81 of those PHIL/RSTD sections and 4,556 SCH**. Their department-owned instruction divides into **43 PHIL sections (2,659 SCH)** and **38 RSTD sections (1,897 SCH)**. They also taught **seven sections (303 SCH)** outside PHIL and RSTD: six HONR sections and one IDST section.

The difference between the complete department portfolio and the current-cohort table is **20 PHIL/RSTD sections and 1,455 SCH**. Those observations are parts of the department's instructional portfolio but are not attributed here to the current 13-person full-time cohort. They can reflect adjunct instruction, instruction by faculty outside the current cohort, or instruction by faculty whose governed home unit lies elsewhere.

## 7. Overall characterization

- Schedule observations establish instructional delivery, not teaching quality or effectiveness.
- SCH is an instructional-volume measure, not a measure of faculty quality, course importance, curricular necessity, or institutional value.
- Section counts include different course types, credit values, enrollments, and instructional forms.
- The current-cohort table does not attribute every PHIL/RSTD section to the 13 current faculty.
- No authoritative same-year governance roster is available; no later roster is substituted.
- FY26 LHE expectations are not evidence of 2023–24 expectations.
- No observed/expected ratio, normalized measure, scalar contribution score, ranking, recommendation, or personnel evaluation is produced.
