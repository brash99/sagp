## 1. Purpose and evidentiary boundary

This report describes the teaching and service contributions that ISO can observe for the Department of Philosophy and Religion during the complete 2025–26 academic cycle. It is descriptive, not evaluative.

The report also presents each faculty member's **FY26 beginning and final LHE expectations** and any explicit adjustment or special role recorded in Carolyn Davis's workbook. The instructional observations and the expectations apply to the same academic year, but they remain different semantic objects. This report places them side by side without dividing SCH, sections, or courses by expected LHE; without calculating observed-to-expected ratios; and without inferring under-teaching, over-teaching, productivity, fairness, adequacy, quality, or institutional value.

Four evidence scopes remain distinct:

1. The **departmental instructional portfolio** includes every observed PHIL and RSTD section in the 2025–26 cycle, regardless of the instructor's current workforce status or governed home unit.
2. The **current faculty teaching table** includes instruction attributed through governed identity resolution to the 13 current full-time department faculty.
3. The **governance-service table** transcribes Philosophy and Religion appointments from the authoritative 2025–26 Academic Standing Committees workbook.
4. The **LHE context table** transcribes institutional expectations and adjustments from the authoritative FY26 teaching-load workbook.

Section and SCH totals are cumulative across the selected fall, spring, May, and summer terms. PHIL/RSTD offerings occur in Fall 2025, Spring 2026, May Term 2026, Summer Term 1 2026, and Extended Summer 2026; no PHIL/RSTD offering is observed in Summer Term II 2026. Independent studies and practica can carry low enrollment or one credit and still count as sections. A governance roster row establishes a published appointment, not attendance, effort, quality, effectiveness, or the absence of other service.

Those observations are parts of the department's instructional portfolio but are not attributed here to the current 13-person full-time cohort. They can reflect adjunct instruction, instruction by faculty outside the current cohort, or instruction by faculty whose governed home unit lies elsewhere. The report does not silently reassign those sections.

## 9. Limitations and non-meanings

- Schedule observations establish instructional delivery, not teaching quality or effectiveness.
- SCH is an instructional-volume measure, not a measure of faculty quality, course importance, curricular necessity, or institutional value.
- Section counts include different course types, credit values, enrollments, and instructional forms. A section is not a standardized workload unit.
- The current-cohort table does not attribute every PHIL/RSTD section to the 13 current faculty.
- The 2025–26 governance table is a direct workbook transcription, not yet a production governance contribution object.
- Governance roster rows do not establish attendance, effort, work performed, quality, importance, or effectiveness.
- LHE expectations are institutional assignments, not evaluative scores.
- No observed/expected ratio, normalized measure, scalar contribution score, ranking, recommendation, or personnel evaluation is produced.
